Location, Time & Device — Verified Instantly

One of the most powerful — and often sensitive — parts of image metadata is geolocation. Toolivo decodes GPS coordinates into human-readable latitude/longitude and displays them in a dedicated map-ready format, while clearly indicating when location data is present (or absent).

Paired with timestamps (capture time, digitization time, modification time) and device fingerprints (camera make/model, lens, firmware), you gain a complete audit trail — essential for legal, journalistic, or archival workflows.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my files uploaded or stored anywhere?

No. All metadata parsing happens locally in your browser using libraries like exif-js. Your images and videos never leave your device — no uploads, no servers, no tracking.

What types of files does it support?

Images: JPEG, PNG, WebP (with EXIF/XMP/GPS).
Videos: MP4, MOV, AVI (basic metadata like duration, resolution, creation date). Support depends on browser capabilities and embedded data.

Can I see GPS location data?

Yes — if your photo contains geotags, Toolivo displays them as latitude/longitude coordinates and clearly indicates when location data is present (or absent). No map rendering, but values are copy-ready for external use.

Why is some metadata missing from my file?

Not all devices or apps embed full EXIF data. Screenshots, social media downloads, or edited files often strip metadata. Toolivo shows everything that’s actually embedded — nothing is hidden or filtered.

Can I export or save the metadata?

Yes. You can copy individual fields or export the full dataset as raw JSON for documentation, forensic reports, or archival purposes — no watermarks or restrictions.

Does it work on mobile phones?

Yes. Toolivo works on iOS and Android browsers. You can inspect EXIF data from photos taken moments ago — ideal for quick privacy checks before sharing.

Is Toolivo really free — forever?

Yes. No signups, no ads, no premium tiers. It’s a public utility for transparency — built for journalists, photographers, and privacy-conscious users.

Can it detect if a photo has been edited?

Indirectly. If modification dates differ from creation dates, or if software tags (e.g., “Photoshop”) appear in XMP data, those clues may indicate editing. However, Toolivo doesn’t perform deep forensic analysis — it only displays what’s embedded.

Troubleshooting

No metadata appears for my image

The file may have had its EXIF data stripped (common with screenshots, social media downloads, or edited images). Try a photo directly from your camera — those usually contain full metadata.

Video metadata is limited

Browser-based video metadata extraction is restricted for security reasons. Only basic fields (duration, dimensions, creation time) are available — not codec details or advanced tags.

GPS data isn’t showing

Many phones disable geotagging by default. Check your camera app settings. Also, iOS and Android often remove location data when sharing via messaging apps — use “original” file transfer methods.
