Troubleshooting

When using a free text to HTML converter online, ensure your syntax follows the supported rules (e.g., ## Subheading, - item). The preview modal shows rendered output for verification.

Conversion feels slow with large files

Processing large documents or using the batch text converter free option may take longer on older devices. Since this free online text converter runs entirely in your browser, speed depends on your device performance.

Download not starting

If downloads do not start, check your browser permissions or disable download blockers. Make sure JavaScript is enabled so the converter can generate and download files properly.
